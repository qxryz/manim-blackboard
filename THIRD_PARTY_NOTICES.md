## Three.js

The interactive 3D player uses three.js r187. Copyright © 2010-2026 three.js authors. The browser bundle also contains polygon triangulation from Earcut, path operations from Paper.js, a distance calculation from Geometric Tools, and shader routines from the LTC reference implementation, Filament, the Khronos Group and IOLITE.
